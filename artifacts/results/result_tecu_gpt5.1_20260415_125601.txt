## RECOMMENDATIONS

- **Required field handling**
  - Implement or surface inline required validation for:
    - First Name
    - Email ID
    - Mobile Number
    - Last Name  
  - Ensure errors are triggered on blur and/or when attempting to proceed, and that they render with a consistent error class (e.g., `.MuiFormHelperText-root.Mui-error`) so automated detection works.

- **Email validation**
  - Strengthen client-side email validation to reject clearly invalid formats like `user@domain` without a TLD.
  - Confirm that the same rules are enforced server-side to avoid inconsistent behavior.

- **Branch dropdown**
  - Investigate the custom dropdown implementation; the listbox was not detectable with standard selectors after programmatic click.
  - Ensure the options container exposes ARIA roles (`role="listbox"` / `role="option"`) or recognizable classes to support automation and accessibility.

- **Accessibility considerations**
  - Confirm that all error messages are associated with their inputs via `aria-describedby` or similar, and announced by screen readers.
  - Ensure the branch selector is keyboard accessible (Space/Enter to open, arrow keys to move, Esc to close) and exposes correct ARIA attributes.

- **Further testing areas**
  - Verify that required validation and email format checks are enforced on form submission/navigation, not only on blur.
  - Regression-test formatting/uppercasing logic (names, mobile mask) across different browsers and devices once validation fixes are in place.
