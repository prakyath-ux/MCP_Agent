- Add **email format validation** with clear messaging (e.g., “Enter a valid email address”) and ensure it triggers on blur/change, not only on submit.

- Accessibility considerations:
  - Ensure all error messages are programmatically associated with their fields (e.g., `aria-describedby`) and announced by screen readers.
  - Confirm that required fields are indicated both visually and via semantics (`aria-required="true"` or similar).
  - For the Branch dropdown and its search input, ensure ARIA roles (`combobox`, `listbox`, `option`) are correctly wired and keyboard-accessible.
- Further testing:
